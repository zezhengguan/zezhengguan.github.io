TaBot is a human-computer interaction design and robotic-like intelligent product design project that explores how an AI-enabled robotic smart meeting table can promote equal dialogue experiences. The system combines multimodal sensor technology with AI algorithms to detect subtle shifts in the atmosphere of multi-person conversations.

The table responds through non-verbal dynamic changes that serve as environmental cues, encouraging participants to become more aware of their conversational behavior and its impact on others. TaBot was developed through a collaboration between X-Group and the Immersive Design Group.
