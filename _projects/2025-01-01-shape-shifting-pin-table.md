Shape-Shifting Pin Table is a reconfigurable motorized pin-array that converts spatial, temporal, and programmatic urban datasets into tangible physical topographies. Created as a dynamic estate scale model and map generation device for CBRE, it enables planners, educators, communities, and other stakeholders to prototype, compare, and communicate scenarios through physical representations of terrain, density, and development massing.

The installation centers on a 10 ft x 10 ft Dynamic 3D Actuator Pin Table with 4,900 independently driven pins. Six HD ultra-short-throw projectors map texture, data, and visual context directly onto the moving surface, while an infrared camera tracking system continuously recalibrates the projection as the terrain changes. The pin-array, projection mapping, curved LED visualization wall, and real-time digital rendering operate as a synchronized system, turning urban analytics into an immediately legible and interactive physical model.

The project was designed and manufactured through a collaboration between Prof. Seungwoo Je's team at the SUSTech School of Design and DCBolt Productions, with CBRE as the client and contributions from Imerza and LightAct. It received the [2026 iF Design Award](https://ifdesign.com/en/winner-ranking/project/shape-shifting-pin-table/740659) in Branding & Communication Design / Data Visualisation and was named a winner in the Red Dot Award: Brands & Communication Design 2026. [View the iF Design Award certificate (PDF)](/images/projects/Shape-shifting_Pin_Table3.pdf) and [the Red Dot Award confirmation (PDF)](/images/projects/Shape-shifting_Pin_Table_RedDot.pdf).
