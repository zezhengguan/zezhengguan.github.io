ThermoShift is a wearable haptic and thermal feedback glove that renders the sensation of temperature moving across the palm. It builds on thermal referral, a cross-modal effect in which nearby haptic and thermal stimuli cause temperature to be perceived at another location. Peltier elements provide thermal stimulation, while Linear Resonant Actuators (LRAs) trace directional paths across the palm. A microcontroller coordinates both feedback channels to produce dynamic thermal illusions without requiring a dense array of thermal actuators.

The evaluation examined how actuator configuration and motion direction affect the perception of thermal movement. Its findings informed the final glove design and showed that participants could perceive the direction and character of thermal motion even when its precise route was difficult to localize. ThermoShift therefore extends hand-based thermal feedback beyond static hot-and-cold states toward continuous, directional thermal-motion simulation for immersive VR experiences.
