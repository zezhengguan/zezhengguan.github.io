Urban design is increasingly becoming an interactive and collaborative process through technologies such as augmented reality. PinCity is a multiplayer AR urban design system that establishes real-time tangible interaction between virtual urban models and a physical pin-array-based shape-changing display within a synchronized augmented environment.

Multiple users can manipulate a shared urban scene while the physical display provides corresponding shape output and tangible feedback. By combining virtual urban models, physical feedback, and multi-user collaboration, PinCity supports intuitive experience co-design for dynamic urban-design scenarios.
